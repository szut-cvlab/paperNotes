### 1. [FlowOVD: Learning Generative Latent Flows for Zero-shot Open-vocabulary Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00782)

### 3. [Self-Improving Small Object Grounding in LVLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01612)
